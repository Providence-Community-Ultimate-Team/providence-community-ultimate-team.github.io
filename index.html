Providence Community Ultimate Team

Welcome to the PCUT website!

Pickup

We play pickup ultimate frisbee games on Sundays at 11am at Hope High School and Wednesdays at 5pm at India Point Park.

Find us on pickupultimate for more information, especially as game times and locations can change regularly.

We hope to see you on the field!

Frisbee Leagues

Rhode Island People's Ultimate League (RIPUL)
RIPUL runs a mixed summer league from May through August. Visit their website for more information, or check out their instagram
Rhode Island Disc League (RIDL)
RIDL runs mixed outdoor spring and fall leagues, as well as two indoor winter leagues each year in Warwick and Tiverton.
4v4 League
The Johnston 4v4 mixed leagues run concurrently with other league in spring, summer, and fall.
