Pickup

last updated: March 23, 2026

The best way to find out when and where we're playing is to join the email list. So send us an email to get added and come play!

We play on Sunday at 11am at Hope High School and Wednesday at 5pm at India Point Park. The location often changes due to field availability, so all possible locations are listed below. During the summer, the Wednesday game often moves to Thursday to avoid conflicting with the RIPUL summer league. Usually, at least someone is there right at the start time to throw, but sometimes people won't show up until 15-20 minutes after the start time, especially for the 5pm games.

The sunday game is outdoor all year, weather permitting. When Daylight Savings Time ends, the weekday 5pm game typically moves to indoor where we play a 4v4 half-court style game. Information about indoor games is available through the email list.

Some things you'll probably want to bring:

  • Water
  • A light shirt and a dark shirt (or a grey if you want to get heckled)
  • Appropriate footwear, recommended:
    • Cleats, for grass fields
    • Cleats or turf shoes, for turf fields
    • Basketball shoes or basketball court appropriate shoes (required for indoor)

You can also find us on pickupultimate.

We hope to see you on the field!

Spring, Fall, Winter

RIDL runs mixed outdoor spring and fall leagues, as well as two indoor winter leagues each year in Warwick and Tiverton.

Spring, Summer, Fall

The Johnston 4v4 mixed leagues run concurrently with the other leagues in spring, summer, and fall. To learn more, join our email list as season announcements are shared there.
